Not modular. Not site-built.

Prefab speed, designed like a custom school.

Modular buildings ship as boxes. Site-built schools take years. Folia sits between them: a pre-engineered system of panels and frames that architects can still design around.

One system, every building

Five building types.
One set of parts.

Classrooms, multipurpose rooms, gyms and science buildings all come from the same frames, panels and glazing. A campus built in phases still looks like one campus.

Plans that change

Move the walls,
not the building.

Interior walls in a Folia building aren’t load-bearing. As programs change, classrooms become suites, labs or a learning commons inside the same shell.
